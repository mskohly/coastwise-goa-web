A practical choice for travellers who want a scenic bay, easy activities and a relaxed sunset without giving up useful facilities.

How to spend a day at Palolem

  1. Morning: arrive around 9:00 and walk the bay before the busiest hours.
  2. Late morning: consider kayaking only with a responsible operator and suitable conditions.
  3. Lunch: look for Goan fish thali, vegetarian xacuti or fresh catch.
  4. Afternoon: explore the quieter end where access is clearly permitted.
  5. Sunset: leave enough time for the return journey.

What to carry

  • Sun protection and drinking water
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Sandals, swimwear and a change of clothes
