Vagator Beach guide

Red cliffs, a fort viewpoint above the sand and one of Goa's best sunset spots, for travellers who prioritise scenery.

How to spend a day at Vagator Beach

  1. Morning: swim while the main stretch is still quiet.
  2. Late morning: walk up to Chapora Fort — ten to fifteen minutes uphill in proper shoes.
  3. Lunch: keep it light at a cliffside café; portions run smaller than beach shacks further south.
  4. Afternoon: relax on the sand, choosing the quieter southern stretch if you want fewer people.
  5. Sunset: watch from the fort viewpoint — one of the most photographed views in North Goa.

What to carry

  • Good footwear for the fort climb
  • Camera or phone charged for sunset
  • Sun protection
