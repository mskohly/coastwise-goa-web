Mobor Beach guide

Where the Sal river meets the sea — Goa's main hub for parasailing, jet-skis and river-mouth boat rides.

How to spend a day at Mobor Beach

  1. Morning: arrive and compare water-sport operators on price and safety equipment before booking.
  2. Late morning: book a parasailing or jet-ski session, confirming total cost and duration in advance.
  3. Lunch: eat riverside — several spots overlook the Sal river mouth.
  4. Afternoon: take a calmer river-mouth boat ride if you want a break from watersports.
  5. Evening: leave extra time for the return journey — traffic can be slower from this stretch.

What to carry

  • Photo ID for water-sport operators
  • Change of clothes
  • Waterproof pouch for valuables
