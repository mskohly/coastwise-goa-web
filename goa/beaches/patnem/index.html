How to spend a day at Patnem Beach

  1. Morning: start on the sand — noticeably calmer than Palolem at the same hour.
  2. Late morning: try kayaking or paddleboarding after checking conditions with a local operator.
  3. Lunch: have a boutique café brunch — Patnem's cafés are known for a relaxed feel.
  4. Afternoon: rest, or take the headland walk toward Rajbag.
  5. Sunset: enjoy fewer crowds than the Palolem sunset experience.

What to carry

  • Sun protection
  • Light layer for breezy evenings
  • Cash — smaller cafés may not take cards
