Calangute Beach guide

Goa's busiest beach, and the one with the widest range of facilities, food and activities within easy walking distance.

How to spend a day at Calangute Beach

  1. Morning: arrive around 8:00 to walk the beach before it fills in.
  2. Late morning: book a water-sports session — compare operators and agree the full price first.
  3. Lunch: look for a beach-shack seafood platter, grilled kingfish or prawns.
  4. Afternoon: browse the nearby shops and cafés if you want a break from the sand.
  5. Sunset: expect company — this is Goa's most visited beach, even at dusk.

What to carry

  • Cash for shacks and vendors
  • Sun protection
  • Patience for crowds
