Benaulim Beach guide

A working fishing beach with dolphin-watching trips and an unhurried pace, well suited to families and older travellers.

How to spend a day at Benaulim Beach

  1. Early morning: book a dolphin-watching trip directly with a boatman on the sand; agree the price first.
  2. Morning: have breakfast at a family-run shack rather than a resort restaurant.
  3. Midday: swim or relax — the water here is generally calm and shallow near shore.
  4. Afternoon: walk into the village — Benaulim's older lanes and church are close by.
  5. Evening: wind down quietly — gentler than the busier South Goa beaches.

What to carry

  • Cash for the boat operator
  • Hat and sunscreen
  • Light snacks for younger children
