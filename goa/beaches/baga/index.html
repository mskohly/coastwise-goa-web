A practical Baga day

  1. Arrive around 10:00 and agree on a meeting point.
  2. Inspect safety gear and choose an established activity operator.
  3. Confirm menu prices before a seafood or Goan sausage lunch.
  4. Use the afternoon for the beach or nearby shopping.
  5. Arrange return transport before the busiest evening hours.

What to carry

  • Light evening layer
  • A pre-agreed transport plan
