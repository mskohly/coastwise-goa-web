Varca Beach guide

Long, uncrowded sand backed by upscale resorts — a calm rather than eventful South Goa beach day.

How to spend a day at Varca Beach

  1. Morning: take a long, quiet beach walk — one of the least crowded stretches in South Goa.
  2. Late morning: have breakfast at a beachside café; non-guests can usually still access them.
  3. Lunch: choose between a resort restaurant or a simpler shack.
  4. Afternoon: swim or rest — calm water and few crowds make for an easy afternoon.
  5. Sunset: expect near-solitude, even in season.

What to carry

  • Sun protection
  • Comfortable resort-casual wear
  • Binoculars if visiting in turtle-nesting season
