Anjuna Beach guide

Cliffside views, a famous flea market and a laid-back backpacker energy, with more character than Goa's busier beaches.

How to spend a day at Anjuna Beach

  1. Morning: have breakfast at one of the cafés perched right above the water.
  2. Late morning: browse the Wednesday flea market, or walk the beach if it isn't market day.
  3. Lunch: try the wood-fired options many beach cafés specialise in.
  4. Afternoon: swim or relax — the northern stretch is generally calmer.
  5. Evening: stay for sunset and the live music that picks up near the cafés.

What to carry

  • Cash for the flea market
  • Comfortable walking shoes for the cliff path
  • Light layer for the evening
