How to spend a day at Arambol Beach

  1. Midday: start late — Arambol runs on a slower clock than most Goa beaches.
  2. Afternoon: swim near the main beach, and ask locally about the rip current near the northern rocks.
  3. Late afternoon: walk to the Sweet Water Lake past the headland, weather permitting.
  4. Sunset: join or watch the drum circle at the northern end — an Arambol tradition.
  5. Evening: have dinner at a budget beach café — long-stay traveller favourites line the main lane.

What to carry

  • Cash (limited card acceptance in places)
  • Light clothing for warm evenings
  • An open schedule — plans here run loose
