Morjim Beach guide

A calmer North Goa option with open sand, café choices and seasonal beginner-friendly surf instruction.

Couples & familiesBeginner surfModerate crowd
Not yet locally verified. Surf availability and sea conditions vary; follow instructors and local safety advice.

How to plan a Morjim day

  1. Start with breakfast near the coast around 8:00.
  2. If surfing, use an established school and follow the instructor.
  3. Recover over a shaded lunch and hydrate.
  4. Take a long walk while respecting protected areas and signs.
  5. Plan to leave before peak evening traffic.

What to carry

  • Reef-conscious sunscreen
  • Rash guard if surfing
  • Reusable water bottle
