Mandrem Beach guide

A calmer North Goa beach with boutique stays and a yoga-and-wellness feel, plus a river backwater for a change of scene.

How to spend a day at Mandrem Beach

  1. Morning: walk the quiet beach — the tide pools near the river mouth are worth an early look.
  2. Late morning: take a short, inexpensive local boat ride across the river mouth.
  3. Lunch: eat at one of the boutique cafés serving light, well-reviewed food.
  4. Afternoon: try a drop-in yoga session — many stays here offer one.
  5. Sunset: watch by the water — calmer and less crowded than most North Goa beaches.

What to carry

  • Insect repellent near the river
  • Reusable water bottle
  • A book — this beach rewards slow time
